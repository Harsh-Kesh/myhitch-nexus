# Business & advertiser guide

Covers `/business/*`. Business and Advertiser share one workspace — either role gets you here, matching how registration itself works.

## Getting started

Register with the **Business** or **Advertiser** role. You land on **Channel** with a real, empty organisation already provisioned — everything you add from here is real unless a screen says otherwise.

## Channel (`/business/channel`)

Your business presence: branding, follower count, recent videos and campaign/lead summary at a glance. Edit your channel's name, tagline, description and links here — changes are real and visible on your public channel page immediately.

## Verification (`/business/verification`)

Enter your organisation's details, including an ABN (Australian Business Number) — the lookup is a real call to the government's free ABN Lookup service, not a placeholder. Submitting puts your organisation into the real admin verification queue; only an admin approval (in `/admin/organisations`) moves your status to Verified. Nothing here self-approves.

## Advertise → Create a campaign (`/business/campaigns/new`)

A six-step wizard covering budget, dates, targeting, creative upload and review. Submitting is real — the campaign is saved and enters the real admin approval queue (`/admin/ads`) exactly like the SRS requires (no advertising goes live without admin sign-off). What's simulated is delivery: there is no real ad server behind this yet, so an approved campaign won't actually serve impressions to real viewers. Use this to review and refine your campaign setup and approval workflow, not to run a live ad flight today.

## Product links (`/business/product-links`)

Add a product with a name, price and URL. These are real, saved links a creator can attach to a specific moment in their video via "Commerce link" during upload — clicks and conversions on a linked product are tracked for real.

## Leads (`/business/leads`)

Real leads generated by clicks on your product links, with a status you can update as you work them (new / contacted / qualified / closed).

## Billing (`/business/billing`)

Illustrative today — there is no real invoicing system behind this screen yet. Use it to see the intended layout, not as your actual source of billing records.

## A note on registration roles

Only Viewer, Creator, Business and Advertiser are fully built out today. Producer, Education and Government exist as role options at registration but don't yet have their own distinct feature set — the requirements spec doesn't yet define what should make them different from a standard Creator/Business account, so building bespoke screens for them now would mean guessing at scope rather than implementing agreed requirements. If your organisation needs one of these roles specifically, that's a product conversation worth having before building it.
